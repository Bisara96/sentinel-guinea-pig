Our Story

Where every cup tells a story

Brew & Bean started with a simple belief: great coffee doesn't need to be complicated. We source beans directly from farmers in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, roast them in small batches, and serve them in a space that feels like home.

Whether you're here for your morning ritual or an afternoon escape, we're here to make your day a little brighter, one cup at a time.
